There’s more to a conversation than its transcript.

A mother sees a difficult day at school in a glance. An experienced doctor notices what a patient leaves unsaid. People read posture, expression and voice alongside the words.

These are patterns described by behavioural science. An LLM given only a transcript misses those signals. Borry makes them available as measured data.

From a recording to evidence.

  1. Measure what happened.

    A meeting, a call or a video. About 200 open audio and video models extract the raw signals.

    Voice and prosody, volume, laughter and hesitations, speaking turns, facial action units from multiple FACS detectors, gaze, and head and body movements.

  2. Put the science to work.

    Our research engine has read 3,252 papers in behavioural science and extracted 2,331 formulas.

    Each formula is stored as data, with its source and page. It is calculated on the recording and labelled direct, approximate or hint.

  3. Let the LLM interpret.

    The LLM reads the measurements and formula results. It describes what happened and when, citing the exact data row behind each claim.

    We bring the signal, not the verdict.
