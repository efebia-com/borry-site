The problem

LLMs get a transcript. People read much more.

Today an LLM is given a transcript. People read posture, faces and voices.

These are patterns that science has described, not magic.

A mother sees a bad day at school in a look.

An experienced doctor notices what the patient did not say.

How it works

From a recording to claims you can check.

01

Record and extract the raw signals

Record a meeting, a call or a video. About 200 audio and video models extract the raw signals:

  • voice and prosody
  • loudness
  • laughter and fillers
  • turn-taking
  • facial action units from several FACS detectors
  • gaze
  • head and body motion
02

Compute the formulas of behavioural science

Our research engine has read about 2,100 behavioural-science papers and extracted over 2,300 formulas, plus about 500 rules that combine facial action units, each stored with its source and page.

Each one is computed on the recording. Each result is labelled:

directapproximatehint
03

An LLM says what happened, and when

An LLM reads all the readings and results and says what happened when. Every claim cites the exact data row it rests on.

«Motion is generally lower between about 26 and 46 s.»video.yolopose.bins#15
